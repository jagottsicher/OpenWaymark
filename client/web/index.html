OpenWaymark verifier

Checks a provenance chain against a node — signature, commitment and inclusion proof, recomputed here, not taken on the node's word. See OWM-9 A11 for why that distinction is the entire point of this page.

Entity trust levels

This page verifies structure and signatures. It cannot tell you whether the underlying claim was true at the point of first capture — no software can (the oracle problem, OWM-9 A4). Source: github.com/jagottsicher/OpenWaymark/client.
